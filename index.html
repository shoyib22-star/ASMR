Hush

Synthesized live · headphones recommended

Make a room
out of sound.

Nine ambient layers — rain, thunderstorm, ocean, wind, birds, snowfall, fire, alpha waves and slow chords. Pick a mode to set the mood, then tune each layer to taste and let it play until morning.

Every layer is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API, so nothing downloads and nothing ever repeats a seam. Press space to play or pause.
